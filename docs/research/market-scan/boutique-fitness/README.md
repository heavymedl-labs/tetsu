# Boutique fitness

Seven studio-software platforms, ordered by relevance: our benchmark first, then the boutique challengers, then the broad incumbents. Start with [Compare](compare.md) for the side-by-side tables. Each profile runs: takeaways for us, at a glance, pricing, strengths and complaints, API, feature map, sources.

- [Mariana Tek](mariana-tek.md): the premium boutique specialist and our benchmark; quote-only and Stripe-only.
- [Momence](momence.md): the modern challenger with a free tier and self-serve API, now owned by Xplor.
- [Arketa](arketa.md): the venture-backed all-in-one reaching into payroll and accounting; wins on support.
- [Walla](walla.md): the premium, focused boutique platform selling retention insight and polish.
- [Mindbody](mindbody.md): the broadest platform and largest two-sided network, now moving down-market at $79.
- [ABC Glofox](abc-glofox.md): the franchise and multi-country specialist inside ABC Fitness, with the best-engineered API.
- [WellnessLiving](wellnessliving.md): the founder-led generalist across many verticals; published prices, locked-in processor.
